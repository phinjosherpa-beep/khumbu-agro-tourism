Geography & Context

Monjo on the Everest Trail

Monjo (2,835 m) sits directly at the entrance gate of the Sagarmatha National Park Buffer Zone, between Lukla and Namche Bazaar along the Dudh Koshi River.

Monjo (Buffer Zone Hub) Key Trail Hubs
Coordinates: 27.7719° N, 86.7214° E • Elevation: 2,835 m

Gateway to the Park

Monjo hosts the Sagarmatha National Park entry check-post, surrounded by protected pine and rhododendron forests.

Acclimatization Elevation

At 2,835 m, Monjo provides an ideal elevation to rest, acclimatize, and nourish before ascending the steep trail toward Namche Bazaar.

Fertile River Terraces

Fed by clean glacial melt, the terraces of Monjo offer rich conditions for highland potatoes, garlic, wild nettle, and alpine herbs.
